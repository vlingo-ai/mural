This paragraph is prepared for the approved actual-cost pricing model. It is excluded from the uploadable listing because the current preview cannot sell top-ups. Recheck the implemented wallet, receipts, fees and refunds before adding it to the full description.

## Paragraph for the full description

Top up when you want to keep talking. Your balance pays for actual AI usage, and Mural shows an estimate of the conversation minutes it can cover. Duration varies with meanings, teaching and topic searches. Before purchase, you see the amount available for AI, Mural's service fee, payment costs and any buffer separately. Remaining free minutes are used first; a top-up adds to your existing balance.

## Required before use

- Voice and teaching charges settle against the purchased value exactly once, including interrupted requests.
- Estimated minutes are labeled throughout the quote, checkout, balance and receipt experience; no fixed duration is promised.
- Quotes preserve the approved default 15% service fee, the current policy version, channel fees, payment buffer and applicable taxes. The example prices used in interface tests are not launch offers.
- Play and Stripe fulfillment, restore, cancellation, pending states, refunds and controlled live payments pass for the launch markets.
- Privacy, Terms and Data safety match the enabled billing records and retention policy.
